What changed on Home: larger timer chips (countdown 19px to about 35px at 1024x640); clock, weather and strip use the shared side margin. Details
  • Timer chips (the main visible change, with timers): name and countdown are larger and scale with the screen. At 1024x640 the countdown is about 35px, up from 19px, and the name about 24px, up from 19px. The strip stops before the weather button, long names end in "…", and the strip is capped above the clock; if timers ever need more rows than fit, they scroll inside it. Paused timers show "Paused" in an outlined tag with a dimmed time. Tapping a chip still opens Timers.
  • Edges: the clock, weather button and timer strip use the same side margin as the other views (16 to 40px; 31px at 1024 wide) instead of 5% of the width (51px). The weather button and strip sit 20px from the top instead of 5% of the height; the clock sits 12px above the navigation instead of 4%.
  • Clock size: still up to 21% of the width, now also capped at 32% of the card's height. On short screens it is slightly smaller (about 205px instead of 215px at 1024x640, 192px instead of 202px at 960x600); unchanged at 1280x800.
  • Not visible here: the card recomputes its height when the window resizes or rotates, so the navigation no longer sits below a stale height. Navigation, date, weather text and background are unchanged.
